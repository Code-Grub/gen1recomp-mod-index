# Dungeon Delvers

Dungeon Delvers transforms Pewter Museum into the entrance to an enormous randomized dungeon beneath Pewter City.

Choose a rental Pokémon and descend through increasingly dangerous floors filled with roaming Pokémon, trainers, traps, treasure, rest stops, and excavation sites. Recover fossil fragments, reconstruct extinct Pokémon, and revive powerful Ancient variants for use in the main game.

## Features

- Large randomized dungeon expeditions beneath Pewter Museum
- Solo and online multiplayer delving
- Rental Pokémon with persistent delve progression
- Roaming Pokémon and trainers instead of traditional random encounters
- Traps, treasure, environmental hazards, and randomized floor layouts
- Rest stops with special services between dungeon regions
- Fossil excavation minigame
- Multi-piece fossil reconstruction
- Revivable Ancient Pokémon with unique traits
- Museum fossil displays that grow with your discoveries
- Reward Point progression and unlockable dungeon keys
- Optional compatibility with PokeSurvive, Mutant Monster Lab, Unique Menu Icons, and expanded Pokémon systems

How deep you can delve depends on preparation, your Pokémon, and whether you know when to turn back.
