# Neo Nursery

Neo Nursery brings a Tamagotchi-inspired Pokémon nursery to Pokémon Crystal.

Hatch Baby Pokémon and care for them over time by managing their needs, building Friendship, treating conditions, playing minigames, and watching their personalities develop. Once they're ready to leave the nursery, your babies can join you in the main game.

## Features

- Tamagotchi-style Baby Pokémon care
- Egg hatching and persistent nursery residents
- Hunger, happiness, cleanliness, sleep, health, and other care needs
- Friendship and individual development
- Interactive care activities and minigames
- Illnesses and other conditions requiring attention
- Day and night behavior
- Nursery alerts and Baby Monitor functionality
- Unlockable reconstructed Baby Pokémon cut from earlier Pokémon development
- Adoption into your normal Pokémon Crystal party or PC
- Optional integration with PokeSurvive and Mutant Monster Lab

Raise your Pokémon from egg to companion and give some long-lost babies another chance at life.
