**Shiny Starters, Gifts, NPC Trades & Day Care Eggs** makes newly received starters, scripted gifts, in-game NPC trades, and bred Day Care eggs shiny in Gen1Recomp.

### What’s New in v1.4.0
- **Emerald support** added
- New independent option for **shiny Day Care Eggs**
- mod name changed to Shiny Starters, Gifts, NPC Trades & Day Care Eggs

### What’s New in v1.3.0
- **FireRed & LeafGreen support** added
- New independent option for **shiny in-game NPC trades**
- Toggle options now also in Launcher menu

### Options (all independent, default ON)
- **SHINY STARTERS** – Makes Oak's and Elm's lab starters, Yellow's starting Pikachu, and Emerald's Birch bag starters shiny.
- **SHINY GIFTS** – Makes other scripted story gifts and gift eggs shiny (Yellow’s later Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle gifts use this) (Crystals odd egg and Emerald's Lavaridge Town egg also use this).
- **SHINY TRADES** – Makes Pokémon received from supported in-game NPC trades shiny.
- **SHINY DAY CARE EGGS** – Repeatable bred eggs collected from the Day Care in Gold, Silver, Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, and Emerald.

Each option controls only its own category.

### Important
- Enable the mod and set your options before receiving the Pokémon or egg
- Fully close and reopen Gen1Recomp after enabling the mod or changing options (unless changing from launcher settings, then no restart required)

### Supported Games
- Red, Blue, Yellow
- Gold, Silver, Crystal
- FireRed, LeafGreen, Emerald

Requires Gen1Recomp **v0.3.5 or later**.

The mod affects newly received starters, scripted gifts, NPC trades & bred Day Care eggs. It does not change wild encounters, trainer Pokémon, or player-to-player trades.
