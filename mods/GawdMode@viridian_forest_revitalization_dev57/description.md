# Viridian Vivarium

Viridian Vivarium turns Viridian Forest into a hands-on conservation and restoration project.

Use the Field Net to carefully hunt Bug and Plant Pokémon in the forest, donate species toward restoration goals, maintain a living Vivarium, cultivate plants and fungi, unlock new conservation equipment, and hunt for rare Shiny and Prime Pokémon.

## Features

- Field Net Pokémon hunting throughout Viridian Forest
- Active catching encounters with WATCH, CATCH, and LEAVE options
- Species-specific conservation donation goals
- Persistent restoration and donation progression
- Living Vivarium for eligible Pokémon
- Interactive Vivarium care and observation
- Plant and fungi cultivation
- Unlockable conservation shops and milestone rewards
- Specialized equipment for advanced catching and restoration
- Shiny hunting systems
- Rare Prime Pokémon
- Expanded activities for Viridian Forest
- Optional Unique Menu Icons compatibility

Catch, cultivate, donate, and restore the ecosystem one species at a time.
